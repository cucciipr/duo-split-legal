# Delete your DuoSplit account

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

This page explains how to delete your DuoSplit account and what happens to your
data when you do. You can start the process from inside the app, or by emailing
us — you do not need to have the app installed to make a request.

Deleting your account is permanent. It cannot be undone.

---

## Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

1. Open DuoSplit and go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and choose **End our space or delete account**.
3. Choose **Delete my account**.
4. Read what will happen, then continue.
5. Sign in again with the same account you used to create it. We ask for this so
   we know the request is really coming from you — deletion is permanent, and we
   would rather be careful.

Once you confirm, your account is deleted right away.

---

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, email us:

**duosplit.app@gmail.com**

Please send your request **from the email address associated with your DuoSplit
account**. DuoSplit accounts are created through Sign in with Google or Sign in
with Apple, so we have no password to check — the email address you send from is
how we confirm the request is yours.

Include the word **"Delete my account"** in the subject line so we can find it
quickly.

If you use Sign in with Apple and chose to hide your email address, send your
request from the private relay address Apple generated for you (it ends in
`@privaterelay.appleid.com`). That is the address linked to your account.

We aim to complete deletion requests within a few days of receiving them.

---

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:

- your name and profile photo;
- your email address;
- the identifier linking your account to Google or Apple;
- all sign-in sessions and refresh tokens, which signs you out everywhere;
- your push notification token, if you had notifications enabled;
- any subscription record held for your account.

If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke the sign-in token that
connected your Apple ID to DuoSplit, so the connection is broken on their side
too.

---

## What stays, and why

This part is unusual, so we want to be direct about it.

DuoSplit is built for exactly two people sharing a record of money between them.
If you have shared a space with a partner, the expenses and settlements in that
space belong to **both** of you — not to you alone.

So when you delete your account:

- **your personal details are removed**, as listed above;
- **the shared record stays with your partner**, and your entries appear as
  **"Former partner"** — with no name, no email, no photo, and nothing that
  identifies you.

We do this because deleting the shared record would erase your partner's
financial history too, and they did not ask for that. They may still need it.

**If you have no partner** — you never shared your space, or you were its only
member — then there is no shared record, and everything is deleted. Nothing is
left behind.

The app tells you which of these applies to you before you confirm.

---

## What we never had

DuoSplit does not connect to your bank, and it never holds or moves money. It is
a record of what two people agreed and what each of them paid — nothing more. So
there are no payment credentials or bank details to delete, because we never had
them.
