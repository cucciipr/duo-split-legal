# DuoSplit — Help & Support

DuoSplit is a shared record of the money two people share: who covered what, how you have agreed to split it, and what is still open between you. It does not move money.

**Need help?** Email duosplit.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

When you write, it helps to include your device (iPhone 15, Pixel 8…), the app version from **Settings → About DuoSplit**, and what you expected to happen.

---

## Getting started

**How do I share a space with my partner?**
Create your space, then send the invite from **Invite partner**. Your partner installs DuoSplit, signs in, and enters the code. Before they join, they will see the split arrangement you have proposed and can either agree to it or adjust it with you.

**Why does my partner get to change the split?**
Because it is a shared agreement, not a setting one person owns. Either of you can change it at any time, and the other person is notified. Neither of you needs the other's approval.

## How splitting works

**What is the difference between the default split and a category arrangement?**
The default applies to everything. A category arrangement is an exception for one kind of expense — for example, one of you covers rent entirely while everything else is split evenly. You set both on the **Our agreement** screen.

**I changed our agreement. Did it change our past expenses?**
No. Every expense keeps the split that applied when it was recorded. Changing the agreement affects new expenses only.

**Why does an expense show a different split from our agreement?**
Either it was recorded before you changed the agreement, or someone set a custom split on that specific expense. Open the expense to see which.

## Balance and settling

**What does the balance mean?**
It is the running difference between what each of you has covered, according to your agreement. It is a record of what the two of you have entered — not a bill, and not something DuoSplit enforces.

**How do I settle up?**
Pay each other however you normally do — DuoSplit never touches money — then record the settlement in the app so the balance reflects it.

**We are always roughly even. Is something wrong?**
No. If you have divided responsibilities so that each of you covers different things, a balance near zero is the arrangement working as intended.

## Privacy and your data

**What can my partner see?**
Everything you record in your shared space, including amounts and notes, plus the balance, settlements, and changes to your agreement. They cannot see your email address or anything outside that space.

**Does DuoSplit connect to my bank?**
No. DuoSplit has no access to any account, card, or payment service, and cannot see your real transactions.

## Ending things

**What happens if we disconnect?**
The shared space becomes permanently read-only for both of you. Nothing is deleted, and neither person can delete it. You will both still be able to look back at your history.

**How do I delete my account?**
**Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.** You will be asked to sign in again, because deletion cannot be undone.

**If I delete my account, does my partner lose our history?**
No, and this is deliberate. Your name, email, and profile picture are permanently removed, but the shared record stays with your partner with your identity replaced by "Former partner". Deleting it entirely would erase their financial history without their consent.

**Can I get my data out?**
Email us and we will send you an export of your data.

## Something is wrong

**The app cannot connect.**
Check your internet connection first. If it persists, email us with your app version — this is the kind of problem we need to fix on our side.

**I found a bug, or something is confusing.**
Please tell us. DuoSplit is built by one person, and a specific report — what you did, what you expected, what happened instead — genuinely gets things fixed faster than anything else.
