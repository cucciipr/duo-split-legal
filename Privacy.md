# DuoSplit Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 August 2026

This policy explains what DuoSplit collects, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate. If anything here is unclear, please ask.

---

## 1. What DuoSplit is

DuoSplit helps two people keep track of the money they share — who covered what, how they have agreed to split it, and what is still open between them.

**DuoSplit never moves money.** It does not connect to your bank, hold funds, process payments, or transfer anything between you and your partner. It is a shared record of what the two of you have agreed, and nothing more.

DuoSplit is for people aged 18 and over.

## 2. What we collect

### Information from your sign-in provider

When you sign in with Google (or Apple, where available), we receive your name, email address, and profile picture link. We do not receive or store your password. We store the profile picture as a link only — we do not copy the image.

### What you and your partner create in the app

- The name of your shared space
- Expenses: amount, date, category, who paid, an optional note, and how it was split
- Settlements you record between the two of you
- Categories you create, and their order
- Your split agreement: your default split and any per-category arrangements
- A record of changes to that agreement, including who made each change and when

## 3. What we do not collect

We want to be specific, because "we value your privacy" means nothing on its own. DuoSplit does **not**:

- connect to any bank, card, or payment account
- see any of your real transactions
- collect your location
- read your contacts, photos, calendar, or messages
- use advertising identifiers or run any advertising
- sell or rent your information to anyone
- use your data to train machine-learning models

## 4. Crash reporting

If the app crashes, we send a technical report to **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.) so we can find and fix the problem.

Before anything leaves your device, the report is filtered to remove personal and financial detail. Specifically:

- amounts, notes, names, and email addresses are stripped
- screenshots and screen contents are never attached
- performance tracing is disabled entirely
- local variable values are not included in the technical stack trace

If our filter cannot process part of a report, that part is discarded rather than sent.

## 5. What your partner can see

This is the part most privacy policies skip, and for a shared-money app it matters most.

When you join a shared space with someone, that person can see:

- your display name and profile picture
- every expense either of you records in that space, including amounts and notes
- the running balance between you
- settlements either of you record
- the split agreement, and a record of changes to it — including which of you made each change

**Anything you record in a shared space is visible to the other person.** If you do not want your partner to see something, do not record it in DuoSplit.

Your partner cannot see your email address, your other spaces, or anything outside the space you share.

## 6. Who else we share with

We share the minimum necessary with:

- **Google** (and **Apple**, where available) — to verify your sign-in. They receive only what is needed to authenticate you.
- **Sentry** — crash reports, filtered as described in section 4.
- **Render** — our servers and database, located in the United States.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where necessary to protect someone's safety or to investigate abuse of the service.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

**If DuoSplit is ever sold or transferred**, your information may transfer with it. Whoever takes it on would be bound by this policy, or would have to tell you before anything changed. We will notify you in the app if this happens.

**Aggregate figures** — counts and statistics that cannot identify you or your partner, such as how many spaces exist or how often a feature is used — may be shared publicly or with others. These never contain your amounts, notes, or names.

## 7. Where your information is stored

Our servers and database are hosted in the United States.

DuoSplit is run by one person. That person can access stored data when maintaining the service, investigating a fault, or responding to a support request — not routinely, and not to read your expenses out of interest. Nobody else has access.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, section 9 applies.

Crash reports are retained by Sentry according to their retention period and are then deleted.

## 9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from **Settings → Danger zone** inside the app. Because deletion cannot be undone, we ask you to sign in again first to confirm it is you.

When you delete your account:

- your name, email address, and profile picture link are permanently removed
- your session and sign-in tokens are destroyed
- if you were not sharing a space with anyone, everything in that space is deleted

**If you shared a space with a partner, the shared financial record stays with them, with your identity removed.** Your entries appear as belonging to a "Former partner" rather than to you by name.

We do this deliberately. The shared history in a space belongs to both people. Erasing it entirely on one person's request would delete the other person's financial record without their consent. Your personal information is removed; the shared ledger is anonymised rather than destroyed.

If you would prefer the shared record to be deleted as well, both of you can disconnect and delete your accounts.

## 10. Ending a shared space

If you disconnect from a partner, the shared space becomes permanently read-only for both of you. Neither person can add to it or delete it. This protects both people's record of what happened.

If you were not sharing with anyone, disconnecting deletes the space and everything in it.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your information, and to object to how we use it.

You can see and correct most of your information directly in the app, and you can delete your account at any time as described in section 9. For anything else, email us at duosplit.app@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in California, we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we do not offer financial incentives for your data.

We will never treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

## 11b. Why we are allowed to process your information

We only handle your information when there is a proper reason to:

- **Because you asked us to** — you gave permission when you created an account and when you chose to share a space.
- **Because we cannot run the service otherwise** — recording expenses, applying your agreement, and keeping your balance are the service itself.
- **Because we have a legitimate interest** — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and fixing faults.
- **Because the law requires it** — in the rare cases described in section 6.

Where you live gives you rights over this, and you can withdraw permission at any time by deleting your account.

## 12. How we protect your information

- All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit
- Our database connection is encrypted
- Sign-in tokens are stored on your device in your operating system's secure storage
- Long-lived tokens are stored on our servers as irreversible hashes, not as readable values
- Access to your space's data requires proof that you are a member of it

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you.

## 13. Children

DuoSplit is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

## 14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that meaningfully affects you, we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app before the change takes effect. Continuing to use DuoSplit after that means you accept the updated policy.

## 15. Contact

Email us at duosplit.app@gmail.com with any question about this policy, or about your information.

We are a one-person operation, so please allow a little time — we aim to reply within 30 days at the outside, and usually much sooner.
